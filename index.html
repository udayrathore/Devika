01 / PROJECT UNDERSTANDING

A stronger digital presence for an established name.

Create a website that reflects Devika's aesthetic, presents her work with intention, and makes it easier for prospective clients to enquire.

Devika Sakhuja's work brings together wedding design, aesthetics and curation, creating distinctive event settings through considered décor and styling. The website should reflect this sensibility while making her work, services and approach easy to explore.

What the website needs to accomplish

01.

Brand Positioning

Present Devika's design approach, studio identity and experience through consistent typography, imagery and page layouts.

02.

Portfolio Presentation

Organise weddings and celebrations into project galleries that show the setting, details and scale of each event.

03.

Audience & Enquiries

Give domestic and international clients a clear way to explore services, share event requirements and contact the team.

04.

Digital Discoverability

Use clear project information, accessible content and a search-engine-friendly structure to support discoverability, including through AI-powered search.

02 / DESIGN DIRECTION

An experience that puts the work first.

The visual direction will draw from Devika Sakhuja's wedding designs, event settings and attention to décor and styling.

Photography will lead the website, supported by typography, page composition and subtle interactions.

Selected celebrations01 / 05

Visual Language

Editorial layouts, clear typography, generous whitespace and a limited colour palette will keep attention on the work.

Photography & Presentation

Layouts will accommodate different image compositions and scales, showing both the overall setting and the details of each celebration.

Navigation & Experience

Visitors will be able to move between the studio story, services, portfolio, individual projects and enquiry form.

Interactions & Details

Transitions, hover states and animations will guide navigation and connect sections without competing with the photography.

What the project includes.

A complete website design and development engagement, from initial planning to launch.

  • Understanding your business, audience and objectives.
  • Reviewing the existing website and available content.

Client enquiry journey

  • Clear navigation between services, projects and contact information.
  • Clear pathways to explore your work and get in touch.

  • Enquiry confirmations and notifications to your team.

Visual Design

  • Custom visual direction, typography and layouts.

Website Development

  • Development of all approved website pages.
  • Editable portfolio projects, images and key website content.
  • Accessible, search-engine-friendly page structure.

  • Browser and functionality testing.

Website Structure & Pages

  • Our Story: Your approach, philosophy and studio identity.
  • Work: Portfolio overview and project categories.

Content & Launch

  • Addition of client-supplied content and images.

06 / TECHNOLOGY

A modern and flexible foundation.

The website will be built to remain fast, accessible and easy to manage, with responsive layouts, optimised image delivery, editable portfolio content and a search-engine-friendly structure.

01.

Website performance

Responsive layouts and optimised image delivery will support fast page loading across devices.

02.

Infrastructure & Media

The website will use a modern, cloud-based infrastructure designed for reliable delivery, efficient image handling and fast page loading.

03.

Editable content

Portfolio projects, images and key website content will be organised into editable sections, allowing Devika to update and expand the website without requiring development assistance for routine changes.

04.

Scalability

The structure can accommodate additional portfolio projects, pages and functionality agreed as the business grows.

09 / TERMS

Working agreement.

01Reviews & Approvals

The engagement includes up to two consolidated design review rounds and two consolidated testing feedback rounds.

One designated representative will coordinate feedback and approvals.

The website structure, page list and designs must be approved in writing before development. Launch will follow written approval of the completed website.

The review limits do not restrict fixes required to deliver the agreed scope. Changes to approved work or additional requirements will be documented and agreed upon separately.

02Scope & Exclusions

The engagement covers one website for Devika Sakhuja, including the agreed portfolio presentation and client enquiry journey.

Additional websites, client portals, online booking, payment collection, CRM implementation and complex workflow automation are excluded unless separately agreed.

The final website structure, integrations and content requirements will be confirmed before development.

03Deliverables & Ownership

Final handover includes:

  • Editable Figma design files.
  • Custom website code and project-specific designs.
  • GitHub repository access.
  • Website editing administrator access.
  • Setup documentation and website editing guide.

Live website editing training is not included unless separately agreed.

Upon receipt of full payment, ownership of the custom website code, design files and project-specific deliverables created by ArtWorksIT transfers to the Client.

Third-party software, libraries, frameworks, fonts, plugins and licensed assets remain subject to their respective licences.

04Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep shared project information confidential and not disclose it to third parties without prior written consent. This obligation continues after project completion.

05Client Responsibilities

The Client will provide:

  • Approved photographs and portfolio material.
  • Relevant brand assets.
  • Access to required accounts and services.
  • Timely feedback and approvals.

The Client is responsible for permissions relating to supplied content and imagery.

Delays in content, access, feedback or approvals may affect the project timeline.

The total project fee is ₹2,00,000 inclusive of GST.

External services and licensed assets are additional unless expressly included.

This agreement is governed by the laws of India.
