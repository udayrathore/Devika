The Objective

A refined digital home for Devika Sakhuja — one that expresses the brand, lets the work take centre stage, and turns inspiration into meaningful client enquiries.

Devika Sakhuja’s work brings together wedding design, aesthetics and curation, creating distinctive event settings through considered décor and styling. The website should bring that same attention to detail to every interaction.

This engagement will create a presentation-led brand website: an immersive introduction, a beautifully organised portfolio, and a clear path from discovering the work to starting a conversation.

Express the brand with clarity and character

Translate the studio’s identity into a cohesive digital language through typography, imagery, layout and thoughtful motion.

Give the work room to tell its story

Present selected weddings and events through curated galleries and project stories, with a browsing experience that feels effortless.

Turn interest into qualified enquiries

Build a clear conversion journey with contextual calls to action and a considered enquiry form that captures the information needed for an initial conversation.

Create a foundation the team can manage

Provide structured content management for approved portfolio and brand content, with an editing guide at handover.

02 / SCOPE & DELIVERABLES

Designed to inspire.
Built to connect.

One complete engagement to design, develop and launch a branding and portfolio website, with a purposeful client enquiry and onboarding flow.

01

Discovery & Strategy

  • Stakeholder discussions and existing website review
  • Brand positioning, audience and website objectives
  • Content hierarchy, sitemap and approved template list
  • Portfolio structure and enquiry requirements
02

Experience & Conversion

  • Clear navigation and mobile-first user journeys
  • Flow from brand introduction to portfolio to enquiry
  • Contextual calls to action across key pages
  • Enquiry form with agreed fields such as event type, date, location and contact details
  • Submission confirmation and enquiry routing to the team
03

Visual Design

  • Digital art direction, typography and layout system
  • Image-led presentation and curated project storytelling
  • Desktop, tablet and mobile layouts for approved templates
  • Reusable UI components and considered interactions
  • Editable Figma files and developer handover notes
04

Website Development

  • Responsive front-end development
  • CMS for agreed portfolio and brand content
  • Project galleries and approved content structures
  • Enquiry form validation, handling and team notifications
  • Performance optimisation and cross-browser testing
05

Brand & Portfolio Pages

  • Home: brand introduction and selected work
  • Our Story: Devika’s approach and studio identity
  • Work: portfolio index and project detail template
  • Client stories or testimonials supplied by the client
  • Connect: enquiry form and contact information
06

Content, Testing & Launch

  • Population of final client-supplied launch content
  • Quality assurance and user acceptance testing
  • Production deployment and domain configuration
  • Repository access, CMS credentials and setup documentation
  • CMS editing guide and one month of post-launch bug fixes

The page list is a proposed starting structure. The final sitemap, templates, form fields, integrations and launch content list will be agreed in writing before development. Onboarding covers the initial enquiry and handoff to the team; a client portal, booking system or CRM is outside this scope unless separately agreed.

Annual hosting & maintenance

Hosting and maintenance are included for the first year from go-live. The annual hosting and maintenance fee applies after the first year. Infrastructure limits, routine maintenance coverage and support response arrangements will be agreed before deployment.

  1. The initial post-launch support period covers bug fixes relating to delivered website functionality for one month after go-live.
  2. A bug is a deviation from the approved design or agreed functionality, rather than a new feature or enhancement.
  3. New functionality, additional modules, major changes and enhancements requested after delivery will be scoped separately.
  4. Hosting configuration and ongoing infrastructure responsibilities will be confirmed before deployment. Third-party services beyond the agreed hosting arrangement will be identified separately.
