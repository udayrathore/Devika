01 / PROJECT UNDERSTANDING

A stronger digital presence for an established name.

Devika Sakhuja has built a recognisable presence in the world of weddings and celebrations, creating distinctive experiences for a discerning clientele.

The website needs to reflect the scale, quality and character of that work. It should reinforce the brand's positioning, present its portfolio with the attention it deserves and offer a considered experience to prospective clients, including those discovering the brand from outside India.

For an audience that may already be familiar with Devika's work, the website becomes an important point of reference: a place to explore the portfolio, understand the offering and take the next step.

What the website needs to accomplish

01.

Brand Positioning

Present a cohesive and recognisable brand experience that reflects the quality of work, creative perspective and clientele Devika Sakhuja represents.

02.

Portfolio Presentation

Create an immersive, well-organised presentation of weddings and celebrations, allowing the scale, detail and individuality of each project to come through.

03.

Audience & Enquiries

Address a discerning domestic and international audience, with a clear path from discovering the work to making a meaningful enquiry.

04.

Digital Discoverability

Build a technically sound website with the foundations for search engine visibility and discoverability across emerging AI-powered search experiences.

02 / DESIGN DIRECTION

An experience that puts the work first.

The visual direction will take its cues from the character of Devika Sakhuja's work: considered aesthetics, distinctive settings and an attention to detail that extends across every celebration.

The website will use photography as its primary visual language, supported by thoughtful typography, composition and restrained interactions.

Selected celebrations01 / 05

Visual Language

An editorial approach to layout, with considered typography, generous whitespace and a restrained visual palette. The interface should feel refined and contemporary without becoming overly decorative.

Photography & Presentation

Photography will lead the experience. Layouts will accommodate different image compositions and scales, giving each celebration room to communicate its own character.

Navigation & Experience

A clear, intuitive structure will allow visitors to move naturally between the brand story, portfolio, individual projects and contact information.

Interactions & Details

Subtle transitions, considered hover states and purposeful animations will introduce depth and continuity without competing with the photography.

09 / TERMS

Working agreement.

01Reviews & Approvals

The engagement includes up to two consolidated design review rounds and two consolidated testing feedback rounds.

One designated representative will coordinate feedback and approvals.

The website structure, page list and designs must be approved in writing before development. Launch will follow written approval of the completed website.

The review limits do not restrict fixes required to deliver the agreed scope. Changes to approved work or additional requirements will be documented and agreed upon separately.

02Scope & Exclusions

The engagement covers one website for Devika Sakhuja, including the agreed portfolio presentation and client enquiry journey.

Additional websites, client portals, online booking, payment collection, CRM implementation and complex workflow automation are excluded unless separately agreed.

The final website structure, integrations and content requirements will be confirmed before development.
